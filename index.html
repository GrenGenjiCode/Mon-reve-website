Welcome

Your dream home in Kerala

Nestled in Engapuzha, Kozhikode, Mon Rêve is a thoughtfully designed two-storey residence where contemporary architecture meets Kerala's warm hospitality.

Signature round windows, teak floors and a private waterfall pool, set among lush greenery. You book the entire villa, so no one else shares the house, the pool or the garden.
